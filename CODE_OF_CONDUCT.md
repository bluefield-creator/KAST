# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever
their background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted advances
- Insults, derogatory comments, trolling, or personal attacks
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Publishing others' private information without their permission
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

Report concerns to the community leaders on the
[3rd Shock Army Discord](https://discord.gg/kWScy7rDm8). Reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Consequences

Leaders decide on a response based on severity and history. Responses range
from a private warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban for repeated or
serious violations.
